Understanding Explainable AI

Grad-CAM

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping highlights image regions that contribute to a model's prediction using gradient information.

LIME

Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations identifies image regions that influence an individual prediction through local approximations.

These visualizations provide model interpretability insights. Highlighted regions should not be treated as proof of food quality or safety.
